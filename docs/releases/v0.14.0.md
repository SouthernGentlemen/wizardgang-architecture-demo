## Known limitations

- Public assurance data is a disclosure-safe projection, not the complete private operational, forensic, treatment, vulnerability-report, risk-acceptance, or incident-investigation record.
- ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 records describe alignment posture only; certification is not claimed.
- WCAG records are criterion-level implementation and evidence observations, not a formal A, AA, or AAA conformance claim or certification.
- Repository evidence resolves only against the exact deployed commit identified by `DEPLOYED_SHA`; no moving-branch fallback is used when deployment identity is unavailable.
- Time-bound observation evidence is valid only through its recorded `validUntil`; stale observations fail validation rather than being silently treated as current.
- Published advisories remain distinct from incidents and may legitimately be empty. An empty advisory dataset does not claim that no vulnerabilities, reports, defects, or investigations have existed.
