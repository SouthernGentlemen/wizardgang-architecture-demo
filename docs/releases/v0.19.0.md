## Scope

Release the completed task-oriented frontend consolidation after the route plan, composable demo boundary, demonstrations, assurance workbench, operations page, and five-destination shell were merged in dependency order.

## Included changes

- DEMO-240: define the task-oriented frontend route plan and explicit retirement contract.
- DEMO-241: make the interactive demo presentations safely composable with scoped IDs, headings, paths, and client behavior.
- DEMO-242: consolidate the Platform and Interfaces browser hierarchies into `/demos` while preserving their machine and protocol endpoints.

- DEMO-244: consolidate assurance, governance, evidence, compliance, risks, incidents, and concerns at `/assurance` with durable fragments.
- DEMO-245: consolidate status, availability, activity, usage, and deployment evidence at `/operations`.
- DEMO-246: finish the five-destination shell, three-action homepage, task-oriented Security page, public-safe reporting link, synchronized localization, compatibility cleanup, and regression coverage.

## Known limitations

- Superseded public browser paths intentionally return ordinary 404 responses without redirects or compatibility aliases; old bookmarks must move to the consolidated destinations and durable fragments.
- The structured manual accessibility matrix remains pending; automated axe and browser checks cover only machine-detectable behavior in the configured route/state matrix and are not certification.
- `/admin` remains private and non-indexable, while `/offline` remains a public but non-indexable recovery surface outside normal navigation.
