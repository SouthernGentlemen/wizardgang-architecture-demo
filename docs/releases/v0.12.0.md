## Known limitations

- Public assurance records are disclosure-safe projections and are not complete private operational, forensic, treatment, or acceptance records.
- No actual incident record is established by this release; that is not a claim that no incident has ever occurred.
- `EX-001` remains planned and simulated, due 2026-12-02; this release does not claim that the exercise has been completed.
- Risk publication does not accept residual risk or upgrade control effectiveness.
- ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, WCAG, and related statements remain alignment claims only; certification is not claimed.
- DEMO-114 and later assurance work are outside this release boundary.
